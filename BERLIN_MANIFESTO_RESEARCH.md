# Berlin 2026 manifesto research: replacing federal-flavored issues

**Research date:** 16 August 2026
**Purpose:** `welchepartei_data.json`'s 15 issues were largely backfilled from the archived **Bundestagswahl 2025** Wahl-O-Mat — the `party_rationale` fields in the current fixture openly admit this (e.g. *"Bundespolitischer Haushalt-Kontext... keine Zusage für Berlin 2026"*). Roughly 8 of the 15 questions ask about things the Abgeordnetenhaus does not decide: Gaza/Ukraine/Trump foreign policy, asylum at the federal border, Bürgergeld, statutory retirement age, federal health-insurance structure, federal school-competency allocation, and company gender quotas.

This document reviews each of the 15 admitted parties' actual 2026 Berlin Abgeordnetenhaus manifestos (Landeswahlprogramme), extracts genuinely state-level policy positions, and proposes a replacement/expanded issue set sourced from real Berlin campaign documents.

## Sourcing method

Official Berlin 2026 Wahlprogramme were located via WebSearch and read either via WebFetch or by downloading the PDF and extracting text with `pdftotext -layout` (WebFetch could not parse several of these PDFs directly — their raw compressed streams were returned instead). Quotes below are short and directly sourced; page/line references come from the extracted text, not the PDF's own pagination.

## Per-party Berlin policy highlights

### SPD
- **Wohnen:** Wants a "rechtssicherer Mietendeckel" via Länderöffnungsklausel or Art. 15 GG; acknowledges "Deutsche Wohnen und Co. Enteignen" reflects majority sentiment but does not commit to full expropriation.
- **Vergesellschaftung:** Cites the referendum as evidence of public will; stops short of committing to implement it as the "Berlin machen bezahlbar" style Linke platform does.
- **Mobilität:** Continue implementing the Mobilitätsgesetz, "gerechtere Verteilung der Verkehrsflächen."
- **Haushalt:** Explicitly "wenden uns gegen die Schuldenbremse... und fordern vom Bund ihre Abschaffung" — opposes it, wants federal abolition.
- **Drogen:** "Drogenkonsumräume bauen wir aus" (expand consumption rooms); "Wir vertreiben drogenabhängige Menschen nicht von Ort zu Ort."
- **Tempelhofer Feld:** "Wir erhalten es in seiner heutigen Funktion."
- **Gesundheit:** New Charité children's hospital; Vivantes/Charité framed as flagship public assets, not remunicipalization-focused.
- **Schule:** Continues Schulbauoffensive "in gleicher Intensität," a Sonderinvestitionsprogramm modeled on it for police/fire infrastructure too.
- **Tierschutz:** Strengthen Veterinärämter, fund Tierheim, cat-neutering programs.
- No explicit facial-recognition/video-surveillance position found (gap).

### Grüne
- **Vergesellschaftung:** Most explicit pro-implementation stance of the major parties: *"Der bereits beschlossene [Gesetzesentwurf] ... eine Vergesellschaftung rechtlich möglich und finanzierbar."* Accuses the CDU/SPD coalition of stalling the referendum's implementation.
- **Mietendeckel:** Wants a durable rent cap via Länderöffnungsklausel or federal rule with sanctions.
- **Sicherheit/Überwachung:** Explicitly rejects "anlasslose Massenüberwachung wie die biometrische Gesichtserkennung"; wants Bodycam use restricted to police, evidence-based sunset review of surveillance measures.
- **Drogen:** Praises the federal Cannabis legalization; wants a **Modellregion für die legale Abgabe von Cannabis** to undercut the black market.
- **Tempelhofer Feld:** *"Ist für uns unverzichtbar – der Volksentscheid ist bindend."*
- **Clubkultur:** Dedicated chapter, "Lebendige Clubkultur – Berliner Clubs schützen."
- **Haushalt:** Wants the (federal) Schuldenbremse reformed/abolished to unlock state investment.
- **Tierschutz:** Dedicated chapter "Echter Tierschutz — konsequent und wirksam."
- **ÖPP:** Cautious — only acceptable if it doesn't create state dependency on single private vendors.

### Die Linke
- **Vergesellschaftung:** Unambiguous: *"[Immo]bilienkonzerne vergesellschaften"*; broader platform theme of "Rekommunalisierung und Vergesellschaftung" across housing, transit, care work, health.
- **Mietendeckel:** New state "Landesamt für Mieterschutz" to enforce it.
- **Haushalt:** *"Die Schuldenbremse erweist sich immer noch als Investitionsbremse"* — wants an investment offensive despite it.
- **Drogen:** Wants Cannabis-Anbauverein authorization simplified; consumption-room language present.
- **Gesundheit:** *"Ausbau unserer kommunalen Träger Charité und Vivantes"* — explicit remunicipalization/public-ownership framing, including bringing outsourced Vivantes subsidiaries back in-house.
- **Schule:** Prioritizes Gemeinschaftsschulen for new builds within the Schulbauoffensive; ~25,000 school places still missing per their own figures.
- **Kita:** Wants Eigenbetriebe (state-run providers) to grow to a third of all Kita capacity.
- **Polizei/Überwachung:** Focus on Bodycam-data transparency/access rights rather than expanding surveillance.
- **Freie Träger:** Wants pay parity between state and freie-Träger employees.

### CDU
- **Wohnen/Vergesellschaftung:** Explicit rejection: *"Wir lehnen planwirtschaftliche Instrumente und die Enteignung von Wohnungsunternehmen strikt ab"* — argues it creates zero new housing and deters investment. Wants an investment-friendly climate for private landlords instead.
- **Sicherheit:** *"Vollständige Videoüberwachung in allen Brennpunktbereichen"*; permanent (non-sunset) legal basis for Bodycams; more mobile/stationary police posts.
- **Integration:** Wants to **abolish quota and funding requirements under the Berlin Partizipationsgesetz** and revoke citizenship from convicted dual nationals for serious crimes.
- **Bildung:** Rejects cheap modular school buildings (MEB) in favor of "architektonisch ansprechende Schulbauten."
- **Haushalt:** Wants to reactivate the debt brake and **anchor it in the Berlin state constitution** for permanence.
- **Gesundheit:** More medical study places (criticizes Charité cuts to same).
- No cannabis/drug-consumption-room, Tempelhofer Feld, nightlife, animal-welfare, or admin-digitalization position found (gaps).

### AfD
- **Wohnen/Vergesellschaftung:** Near-identical wording to CDU's rejection of expropriation ("planwirtschaftliche Instrumente... strikt ab").
- **Mobilität:** Wants to overhaul/roll back the Mobilitätsgesetz, calls its "einseitige Verkehrswende" focus wrong; favors car infrastructure.
- **Sicherheit:** Same "vollständige Videoüberwachung in Brennpunktbereichen" language as CDU, plus stronger Verfassungsschutz monitoring of Islamist extremism and harsher penalties for critical-infrastructure attacks.
- **Integration:** Same Partizipationsgesetz-quota-abolition and dual-national citizenship-revocation demands as CDU, plus opposing "Diversity/Queer/Gender" content in teacher training.
- **Tierschutz:** Notably substantive — criticizes chronic Tierheim overcrowding and understaffed Veterinärämter, frames animal welfare as a public duty currently unmet.
- **Haushalt:** Same debt-brake-reactivation-and-constitutional-anchoring position as CDU.
- No Tempelhofer Feld, cannabis, or nightlife position found (gaps).

### FDP
- **Wohnen/Vergesellschaftung:** Strongest rejection of all parties, framed as constitutional: *"Versuchen den Art. 15 GG anders zu interpretieren, stellen wir uns aktiv entgegen"* — actively opposes reinterpreting the constitutional expropriation clause.
- **Verwaltungsdigitalisierung:** Most concrete proposal of any party — AI system for real-time Bürgeramt slot optimization with proactive waitlist notifications.
- **Kita:** Wants funding parity for freie Träger (80% of Berlin's Kita places) with state-run ones, plus a cross-district "Fast-Track" single-permit system.
- **Clubkultur:** Dedicated chapter "Am Beat der Zeit – Clubkultur stärken."
- **Sicherheit:** More nuanced than CDU/AfD — wants to *evaluate* "anlasslose Videoüberwachung" and scale it back if unjustified; wants to merge the Berlin/Brandenburg Verfassungsschutz agencies for efficiency; wants to weaken the LADG's Beweislastumkehr (burden-of-proof reversal) as bureaucratic friction for police.
- **Haushalt:** Wants the debt brake kept without workaround "Ausweichhaushalte," a spending cap, and priority for private investment.
- **Gesundheit:** Wants Charité to reach "europaweit... Spitze" status including integration of the Deutsches Herzzentrum, "ohne dabei die Zusammenarbeit mit privaten Partnern zu vernachlässigen."
- **Tempelhofer Feld:** Wants to use *unbuilt* parts of the field for regulatory "Experimentierklauseln/Sandboxes" — a notably different framing from the preserve-as-is consensus of Grüne/SPD/Linke/ÖDP, though not explicit building development.

### BSW
- **Wohnen/Vergesellschaftung:** Wants to implement the 2021 referendum outcome; explicit fallback: *"Als letztes Mittel bleibt auch die Vergesellschaftung vorbehalten."*
- **Mobilität:** Rejects the car-vs-bike framing outright — wants both a safe bike network and good car-traffic conditions simultaneously.
- **Sicherheit:** Supports targeted (not blanket) video surveillance at crime hotspots with parliamentary approval; opposes Verfassungsschutz "Gesinnungsprüfung" of public servants and its political misuse.
- **Schule:** Wants primary schools refocused on reading/writing/arithmetic; bans smartphones/tablets in primary classrooms.
- **Kita:** Mandatory attendance from age 4, framed as language/integration policy.
- **Gesundheit:** "Gemeinwohlorientierte Krankenhausplanung" with citizen involvement; wants outsourced Charité/Vivantes subsidiaries re-integrated; opposes DRG flat-rate hospital financing.
- **Tierschutz:** Wants to restore the abolished Landestierschutzbeauftragte position.
- Gaps: no cannabis/drug-consumption-room, LADG/queer, debt-brake, or Tempelhofer Feld/nightlife position found.

### Volt
- **Wohnen:** Explicitly **rejects both blanket rent caps and expropriation**: *"Wir lehnen starre Mietendeckel und pauschale Enteignungen ab, da die Evidenz zeigt, dass sie das eigentliche Problem verschärfen... binden im Fall von Enteignungen Milliarden an Entschädigungen, die für den Neubau fehlen."* Prefers a narrowed Mietpreisbremse plus a large public/cooperative-housing "price anchor."
- **Haushalt:** Own fiscal-discipline proposal — binding 3-year expenditure ceiling, offset-required new spending, independent Fiscal Policy Council audit — distinct from the federal debt-brake framing entirely.
- **Cannabis:** Genuinely Berlin-implementation-level position — wants simple, digital implementation of the federal Konsumcannabisgesetz for growers' associations.
- **Queer/Antidiskriminierung:** Wants to deepen the Landesstrategie für queere Sicherheit, LSBTQIA* liaisons in the Staatsanwaltschaft.
- **Clubkultur:** Backs the "Agent of Change" principle so clubs aren't bankrupted by soundproofing costs from new neighboring development.
- **Verwaltungsdigitalisierung:** Centerpiece "Once-Only" data-reuse principle and Genehmigungsfiktion (auto-approval after deadline), modeled on Estonia.
- Gaps: no explicit facial-recognition/video-surveillance or Vivantes/Charité-ownership position found.

### ÖDP
- **Mobilität:** Chapter titled "Mobilitätswende jetzt – Mobilitätsgesetz umsetzen, ÖPNV ausbauen" — wants the existing law actually implemented.
- **Klima:** Wants Berlin climate-neutral **before 2045**, explicitly faster than the Senate's current target.
- **Tempelhofer Feld:** Strong, explicit — preserve whole per the 2014 referendum, protect as habitat.
- **Tierschutz:** Signature issue — reduce meat consumption, top animal-welfare standards, Berliner Forsten as dual CO₂ sink/recreation space.
- **Wohnen:** General fair-rent language, no explicit Mietendeckel or Vergesellschaftung position (a genuine gap, not an omission).
- Gaps: no cannabis, hospital-ownership, nightlife, debt-brake, or explicit surveillance position found.

### Tierschutzpartei (PARTEI MENSCH KLIMA TIERSCHUTZ)
- Broadest program of any party this cycle by the party's own description — 26 topic areas.
- **Wohnen:** Binding rent cap specifically for **state-owned** housing.
- **Mobilität:** Free public transit, expanded bike-lane network.
- **Klima:** Joint position with Klimaliste — climate-neutral by 2035.
- **Tierschutz (deep, signature issue):** Phase-out plan for animal testing, pigeon houses in every district, more equitable veterinary care, permanent shelter funding, mandatory dog-owner licensing ("Hundeführerschein"), a wildlife competence center.
- **LGBTQ/Barrierefreiheit:** Better protection for queer people; more public accessibility.
- Gaps found in this pass: explicit surveillance, cannabis/drug-policy, Kita, integration, and Tempelhofer Feld positions were not surfaced from the summary page (likely present in the full 26-topic PDF, not yet deep-extracted).

### die urbane. – eine HipHop Partei
- Framing throughout is explicitly "antikolonial & machtkritisch" (anti-colonial, power-critical) — this itself is a distinguishing party signature.
- **Wohnen/Vergesellschaftung:** Full support for "Deutsche Wohnen & Co enteignen," below-market compensation; vacant units >3 months face expropriation/municipal management; immediate closure of collective homeless shelters in favor of guaranteed individual housing.
- **Mobilität:** Free transit funded via CO2/inheritance/wealth tax; amnesty for fare evasion ("Schwarzfahren"); Tempo 130 on highways.
- **Drogen:** Full decriminalization of possession/use of all drugs, legal home cultivation, opposes prohibition as having racist/colonial roots, cites discriminatory enforcement against BIPOC.
- Several further topic pages (climate, education, FLINTA*, LGBTQIA+, digitalization, UBI) exist but weren't deep-extracted in this pass.

### DKP
- No current dedicated Wahlprogramm found (only a 2021 PDF, not verified current); positions below are from 2026 campaign pages.
- **Wohnen:** Opposes "Mietwucher," demands "Enteignung der Immobilienkonzerne" — directly maps to the Vergesellschaftung cleavage.
- **Haushalt:** *"Wir brauchen Geld für unsere Stadt statt Waffen für den Krieg!"* — frames Berlin budget priorities against federal/NATO defense spending.
- **Bildung:** Opposes Bundeswehr cooperation agreements with schools.
- Gaps: climate, surveillance, cannabis, childcare, migration, LGBTQ+, green space, nightlife, animal welfare, digitalization — none found.

### SGP

- **Mobilität:** Criticizes the S-Bahn Berlin re-tender/privatization split between Siemens Mobility and Stadler Rail "zur Profitmaximierung."
- **Wohnen:** Historical critique of the privatization of 150,000 Berlin public-housing units — backward-looking, not a 2026 proposal.
- Gaps: climate, surveillance, cannabis, childcare, migration/integration, LGBTQ+, green space, nightlife, animal welfare, digitalization — none found.

## Cross-cutting finding: the sharpest genuine Berlin cleavage isn't in the current fixture at all

Every party researched — including four of the smallest ones (DKP, die urbane., Tierschutzpartei, PdF's animal-welfare fragment) — takes a position on **Vergesellschaftung** (state expropriation of large housing companies under Art. 15 GG, per the binding-but-unimplemented 2021 "Deutsche Wohnen & Co. enteignen" referendum). Positions split sharply and are richly documented:

- **Pro-implementation:** Linke, die urbane., DKP (unconditional); Grüne, BSW (explicit, with BSW framing it as "last resort")
- **Explicit rejection, framed as unconstitutional/uneconomic:** CDU, AfD, FDP
- **Explicit evidence-based rejection (distinct rationale from CDU/AfD/FDP):** Volt
- **Acknowledges public will, stops short of commitment:** SPD
- **No position found:** ÖDP, Tierschutzpartei (in this pass), SGP, HEIMAT, PdF

This is a sharper, more Berlin-specific cleavage than the current generic "rent control vs. free market" framing of `housing-rents`, and deserves its own issue.

## Data gaps to carry into the fixture, per the project's own methodology

Per RESEARCH.md §C ("a non-response is 'not answered,' never a silent midpoint") and the sparse-profile UX convention, the following should show as explicit gaps rather than inferred positions once the fixture is rebuilt:

- CDU: no found position on cannabis/consumption rooms, Tempelhofer Feld, nightlife, or animal welfare.
- AfD: no found position on Tempelhofer Feld, hospital ownership, or nightlife.
- SPD, Volt: no found position on facial recognition/video surveillance specifically.
- BSW: no found position on climate-neutrality bindingness, the Berlin constitutional debt brake, LADG, or the Partizipationsgesetz.
- Tierschutzpartei: this pass only extracted the summary page and the sections greped directly; the full 26-topic PDF likely has more (Tempelhofer Feld, Partizipationsgesetz not yet confirmed either way).

**Genuinely thin, not just under-researched** (confirmed by direct exploration of each party's official channels, not just search results):

- **PdF (Partei des Fortschritts):** confirmed via direct fetch that their Berlin election page still reads *"Unser Wahlprogramm – Kommt bald. Versprochen!"* — no Berlin-specific program exists to find. The only usable material is their national platform's brief animal-welfare fragment.
- **HEIMAT:** confirmed via search that HEIMAT is running only **district lists (Bezirkslisten)** in 2026, not a citywide state list — unlike SPD, CDU, Die Linke, and the 12 admitted state-list parties. This structurally explains the absence of a state-wide Landeswahlprogramm; it isn't a research gap so much as a fact about their ballot strategy.
- **DKP:** the earlier pass missed an actual dated program — `berlin.dkp.de/wp-content/uploads/sites/83/2026/04/Wahlprogramm.pdf`, an 8-page "Wahlprogramm zur Berliner Abgeordnetenhauswahl 2026" from April 2026. It covers housing (Mietendeckel and uncompensated expropriation), transit (free public transport, opposes S-Bahn privatization and the A100 highway), hospitals (opposes profit-driven hospital management and closures), policing (opposes the December 2025 Sicherheits- und Ordnungsgesetz and surveillance, names Görlitzer Park specifically), and school investment (€200M reversal of cuts, smaller classes). Still no position on climate-neutrality bindingness, the Partizipationsgesetz, the LADG, or admin-digitalization/animal-welfare — genuine gaps in an otherwise real program, not absence of one.
- **SGP:** no Berlin-specific Wahlprogramm exists; their material comes from campaign articles on gleichheit.de/wsws.org. These did yield genuine, specific Berlin positions on housing (explicitly rejecting rent caps as "cosmetic," demanding uncompensated expropriation instead — a rejection from the left, not the market-liberal right) and budget (opposing named Senate cutback figures for transit/hospitals/universities), but nothing on hospital ownership, schools, climate bindingness, or integration law specifically.
- **die urbane.:** turned out to be far from thin — the party runs 33 individual topic pages, and directly fetching six of them (climate, digitalization, antidiscrimination, education, health, and the original housing/mobility/drugs pages) surfaced strong, specific positions on 10 of the 13 issues, including the most aggressive climate target of any party (net-zero within one legislative term) and an explicit cap on private hospital ownership (max 49%).
